<!-- ── BIO ──────────────────────────────────────────────────── -->
<section class="cv-section cv-bio">
  <p>
    I am a neuroscientist and research software developer working at the intersection
    of brain imaging, statistics, and open science. My work focuses on developing
    robust, reproducible analysis methods for MRI and M/EEG data, contributing to
    community-wide standards such as <a href="https://bids.neuroimaging.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">BIDS</a>,
    and creating open-source toolboxes used internationally.
    I held academic and scientific management positions in Edinburgh (2007–2020) before
    moving to Copenhagen to focus on research software engineering and large-scale
    open neuroimaging initiatives.
  </p>
</section>
