# Dr Cyril Pernet

**Senior Research Software Developer**  
Neurobiology Research Unit, Rigshospitalet, Denmark  
Department of Health Technology, Danish Technical University, Denmark

[Publications](/publications.html) . [Contact](/contact/)

---

I am an associate professor in translational neuroimaging working at the intersection of brain imaging, statistics, open science and clinical applications. My work focuses on developing robust, reproducible analysis methods for MRI, PET and EEG data, and applying them to patient populations. I contribute to community-wide standards such as [BIDS](https://bids.neuroimaging.io/), and created [PublicnEUro.eu](https://publicneuro.eu/).
