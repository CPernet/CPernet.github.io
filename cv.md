Associate professor in translational neuroimaging working at the intersection of brain imaging, statistics, and clinical applications. My work focuses on developing robust, reproducible analysis methods for MRI, PET and EEG data, and applying them to patient populations. I am also interested in open science and contribute to community-wide standards such as [BIDS](https://bids.neuroimaging.io/), and created [PublicnEUro.eu](https://publicneuro.eu/).

[Publications](/publications.html) . [Contact](/contact/)

## Selected Leadership & Standards Roles

**2020 --**: **Co-lead -- [Open Brain Consent](https://open-brain-consent.readthedocs.io/en/stable/) (GDPR)**
GDPR-compliant consent templates for open neuroimaging data sharing.

**2019 -- 2020**: **Co-chair -- [OHBM COBIDAS MEEG](https://cobidasmeeg.wordpress.com/)**
Organisation for Human Brain Mapping committee on best practices for M/EEG. Guidelines published in [*Nature Neuroscience* (2020)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-020-00709-0).

**2018 -- present** -- **[Brain Imaging Data Structure (BIDS)](https://bids.neuroimaging.io/)**
Past steering group, colead for [EEG extension](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-019-0104-8) lead, lead the [Genetics extension (GigaScience, 2020)](https://academic.oup.com/gigascience/article/9/10/giaa104/5928221), contribute the various BEPs. International standard for organising neuroimaging data.
